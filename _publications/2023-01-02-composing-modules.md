We investigate the composition of parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations, enabling flexible and efficient model adaptation without full model fine-tuning. Our approach demonstrates that arithmetic operations on parameter-efficient tuning modules can effectively combine and transfer capabilities across different tasks and models.
