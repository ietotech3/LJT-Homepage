We present SynLogic, a novel framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. Our approach generates high-quality logical reasoning data that can be used to train and improve language models in reasoning tasks. Experiments demonstrate that models trained with SynLogic data achieve significant improvements on logical reasoning benchmarks and generalize well to beyond logical reasoning tasks.
