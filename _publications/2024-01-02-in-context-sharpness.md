We propose In-Context Sharpness, a novel approach for hallucination mitigation that leverages the inner representations of language models. Our method uses the sharpness of in-context representations as an effective alert mechanism for detecting hallucinations. Experiments demonstrate that In-Context Sharpness can effectively identify and mitigate hallucinations in large language models.
