In this work, we investigate the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) specifically for chart understanding tasks. We identify key limitations in how current VLMs process and understand visual information from charts, and propose solutions to address these bottlenecks. Our findings provide important insights for improving VLM performance on chart-related reasoning tasks.
