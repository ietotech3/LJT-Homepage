We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models, covering 52 diverse disciplines across four difficulty levels. C-Eval provides a rigorous benchmark for evaluating the knowledge and reasoning capabilities of foundation models in Chinese. The suite has been widely adopted by the community and used to evaluate numerous prominent Chinese LLMs.
