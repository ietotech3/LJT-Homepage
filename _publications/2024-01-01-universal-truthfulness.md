We discover a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models that captures the direction of truthfulness in the model's internal representations. Through systematic analysis, we demonstrate that this hyperplane is consistent across different model layers and can be used to detect and mitigate hallucinations. Our findings provide new insights into the inner workings of LLMs and their representations of truthfulness.
